# NrollXpert: Simple Application, Seamless Enrolment 🚀
> A User-Centric Mobile App Prototype Enhancing the Higher Education Application & Enrolment Experience.

NrollXpert is an end-to-end SDLC project delivered via a highly structured Agile framework. The primary goal was to plan, manage, and deliver a high-fidelity, clickable prototype designed to solve critical friction points in student enrollment processes. 

---

## 👥 Team & Timeline
- **Role:** Scrum Master
- **Team Size:** 10 Cross-Functional Members (UX/UI Designers, Business Analysts, and Quality Assurance)
- **Project Duration:** Oct 27, 2023 – Jan 12, 2024 (4 Completed Sprints)

---

## 📊 Iterative Sprint Breakdown & Architecture
The project was managed across 4 dedicated sprints with a strict focus on logical user-journey progression and iterative testing:

* **Sprint 1 (20/11/2023 - 04/12/2023) | Discovery & Entry**
    * **Sprint Goal:** Allow the applicant to browse programs and choose the desired subject.
    * **Key Focus:** Requirements gathering, building the interactive Home Page, and initializing test cases.
* **Sprint 2 (05/12/2023 - 18/12/2023) | Authentication & Core Support**
    * **Sprint Goal:** Allow the applicant to log in and add support functionality (chat/FAQ/guides).
    * **Key Focus:** Streamlined Create-Account user journeys, system requirements refinement, and user interface testing.
* **Sprint 3 (19/12/2023 - 01/01/2024) | Application Process & Communication**
    * **Sprint Goal:** Allow the applicant to finish their application and see the admission results.
    * **Key Focus:** E2E chat threads implementation, program-specific chat testing, and UI refinement based on stakeholder feedback.
* **Sprint 4 (02/01/2024 - 11/01/2024) | Testing, Hardening & Closure**
    * **Sprint Goal:** Finish the prototype and Testing and organize all the documents.
    * **Key Focus:** SRS verification, compliance checks, defect logging (e.g., UI formatting bugs), and final presentation readiness.

## 🛠️ My Role & Impact as Scrum Master
As the Scrum Master, I **guided the team in using** Agile principles to ensure continuous value delivery and team alignment across the 11-week lifecycle:

* **Ceremony Facilitation:** Managed and facilitated all core Scrum events: Sprint Planning, Daily Scrums, Sprint Reviews, and Sprint Retrospectives.
* **Backlog Refinement:** Collaborated closely with the team to split Epics into actionable, testable User Stories (with clear acceptance checklists as seen in our Sprint test cards).
* **Impediment Removal & Defect Management:** Tracked cross-functional dependencies, handled bugs (such as country title bugs in Sprint 4), and protected sprint capacity.
* **Metrics & Transparency:** Maintained an audit trail of completed work and testing requirements to ensure high product quality by the time of the MVP release.
