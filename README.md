## My Role – Scrum Master
As the **Scrum Master** for this project, my responsibilities included:  

- Facilitating daily scrums, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives  
- Ensuring the team adhered to Agile principles and Scrum framework  
- Helping the team prioritize tasks and maintain a healthy product backlog  
- Tracking sprint progress and resolving impediments  
- Documenting all Agile artifacts in Confluence and Trello for transparency  

This experience demonstrates my ability to lead a team using Scrum
